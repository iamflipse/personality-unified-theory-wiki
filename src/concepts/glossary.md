# Glossary

His labels, as these lectures use them.

- **State.** What a person already is.
- **Transformative process.** What a person is becoming. He treats this as consciousness.
- **Call and conscience.** The two variants of the quest motif.
- **Royal road.** His quotation of the dream phrase, read toward Jung.
- **Shadow.** The part of the pattern of human action you have not yet made explicit. Fiction is one way he says that part becomes sayable.
- **Harmony of person and structure.** The case, in the Rogers lecture, that mental health is not only inside one body.
- **Hierarchy of conceptualization.** His way of stacking Piaget, the biological and behavioral traditions, and the trait measures.
- **Big Five.** Extraversion, neuroticism, agreeableness, conscientiousness, openness. He calls the model so-called and well-known.
- **Positive emotion.** The sign of movement toward an aim, which he ties to dopamine incentive reward.
- **Aim and frame.** What he says the hypothalamus sets.
