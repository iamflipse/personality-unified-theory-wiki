# Dialogue

**Dialogue as Transformation puts Rogers with the existentialists and the phenomenologists.**

He says the lecture concentrates on Carl Rogers and the humanists, and weaves in those two neighboring fields, psychologically and sociologically. The sociological point is the one he flags.

> "a harmony necessarily exists between the individual and the social structure."
>
> [Dialogue as Transformation](lectures/dialogue-as-transformation.md).

He calls that case important because it changes the picture of mental health. Mental health, he says, is easy to treat as a private biological thing inside one person. If the person and the structure have to fit, health is also that fit.

He says "you could make the case." It is his case in the lecture, offered as a case.
