# Aim and Emotion

**Positive emotion, in Traits and Transformation, means you are getting closer to the aim.**

He starts from a person always going from point A to point B, seeing a path and seeing tools.

> "That's what positive emotion is. It's something that signifies movement towards a desired aim, incentive reward that's mediated by the dopamine system."
>
> [Traits and Transformation](lectures/traits-and-transformation.md).

The aim itself, in the same stretch, is placed in the hypothalamus.

> "the hypothalamus sets up the aims and the frame"
>
> [Traits and Transformation](lectures/traits-and-transformation.md).

He calls the other side of the hypothalamus the side that mediates exploration, and he brings up decorticate cats as the example. The page stops at the claim: emotion marks progress toward an aim, and the aim is set with a frame.

The Human Experience lecture says the same landscape in two systems. One orients you to what moves you toward the goal. One orients you to what gets in the way and was not expected. He says that landscape is constant enough that biology adapted to it, so the feelings are not only private. Your brain, he says, adapted to that world, which is why the emotional layout can be treated as part of the environment.
