# Call and Conscience

**The quest, in Personality and Its Transformations, comes in two forms.**

> "The quest motif has two fundamental variants. One is call, the other is conscience."
>
> [Personality and Its Transformations](lectures/personality-and-its-transformations.md).

The example he reaches for is Bilbo and Frodo. He describes them as small, not famous, not especially powerful or wise, living in a protected corner they do not know is protected, while darker forces still apply. He calls that the existential condition of the default human being.

Call and conscience are the two ways that condition turns into a journey. The lecture uses the stories as the pattern. It does not grade the novels.

This is the narrative form of [State and Process](state-and-process.md). State is the corner you already inhabit. Process is leaving it, by a call from outside or by conscience from inside.
