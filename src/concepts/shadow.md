# The Shadow

**What you cannot yet say about people is still in the pattern of how they act.**

Integrating the Shadow opens on the dream phrase and calls it a stellar realization.

> "Dreams as "the royal road to the unconscious.""
>
> [Integrating the Shadow](lectures/integrating-the-shadow.md).

The reading he offers next is that information is coded in the way people interact. You understand some of it. The whole pattern of action you have lived through is larger than what you can state. That, he says, is why fiction pulls you. Watching a representation of behavior widens the part you can say. He ties that picture to Jungian analysis.

The shadow, on this page, is that remainder: motive and action you have already met, and have not made explicit. The lecture is the long form. This page keeps the opening claim.
