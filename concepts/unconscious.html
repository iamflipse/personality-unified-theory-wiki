The Unconscious

Navigating the Unconscious is the analytic lecture. Freud first, then Jung.

"his most brilliant colleague, Carl Jung, who extended and, I think, improved Freud's magnificent contribution."

Navigating the Unconscious.

He says Freud is now a whipping boy, especially for behavioral psychologists, partly because the discoveries people still use have become ordinary, so only the mistakes stay visible. He says an educated person already takes those discoveries for granted. The sentence is his judgment, not a survey.

Adler is in the room and then set down.

"Alfred Adler, as well as detailing the history of the idea of the unconscious before Freud formalized it"

Navigating the Unconscious.

He says the unconscious was speculated about before Freud gave it a formal shape, and that this lecture will spend time on that history. He also says Adler will not get much time. This book does not invent the Adler section he skipped.
